Mounjaro

Mounjaro Questions

Mounjaro (tirzepatide) is an injectable prescription medication that activates both GIP and GLP-1 receptors. It helps regulate blood sugar, reduces appetite, and slows digestion - leading to significant weight loss in clinical studies. It's taken once weekly via a pre-filled pen.

When you contact our team, we'll walk you through our qualification process. We have internal systems to assess eligibility based on your health profile and goals. Simply reach out and we'll handle everything.

We carry all standard Mounjaro dosages: 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 7.5 mg, 10 mg, 12.5 mg, and 15 mg. Our team will help determine the right dose for you. Most patients start at 2.5 mg and titrate up over time.

The most common side effects include nausea, diarrhea, decreased appetite, vomiting, and constipation. These are typically mild and tend to decrease as your body adjusts. Always consult your healthcare provider if you have concerns.

Store unopened Mounjaro pens in the refrigerator at 36-46°F (2-8°C). Do not freeze. An in-use pen can be stored at room temperature (up to 86°F / 30°C) for up to 21 days. Always check the expiration date.

Wegovy

Wegovy Questions

Wegovy (semaglutide) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist specifically approved for chronic weight management. While Mounjaro targets both GIP and GLP-1 receptors (dual-action), Wegovy focuses on GLP-1 alone. Both are effective - our team can help you determine which is the better fit for your goals.

Simply contact our team through the website. We'll assess your eligibility through our internal qualification process and guide you through everything from dosage selection to delivery.

We carry Wegovy in 1.7 mg and 2.4 mg maintenance doses. Dosing is gradually increased over 16-20 weeks to reach the maintenance dose, and our team will guide you through the full schedule.
