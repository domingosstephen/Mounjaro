Understanding Mounjaro

How Mounjaro Works

A quick, clear breakdown of the science behind the medication.

Dual-Action Formula

Mounjaro activates both GIP and GLP-1 receptors - two key hormones that regulate appetite and blood sugar. This dual mechanism is what sets it apart from single-action alternatives.

Slows Digestion

The medication slows food leaving your stomach, keeping you feeling full longer. This naturally reduces calorie intake without extreme dieting.

Gradual Dosing

You start at 2.5 mg and gradually increase over weeks. This titration schedule minimizes side effects and lets your body adjust comfortably.

Proven Results

Clinical trials showed patients losing up to 22.5% of body weight. It's taken once weekly via a simple pre-filled pen - no mixing or measuring required.

Understanding Wegovy

How Wegovy Works

A GLP-1 receptor agonist specifically approved for chronic weight management.

Targets Appetite Control

Wegovy mimics a natural hormone called GLP-1 that targets areas in the brain responsible for appetite regulation, helping you feel fuller sooner and for longer.

Reduces Calorie Intake

By slowing stomach emptying and reducing hunger signals, Wegovy naturally helps you consume fewer calories without extreme dieting or restriction.

Once-Weekly Injection

Simple pre-filled pen taken once a week. Dosage is gradually increased over 16-20 weeks to minimize side effects and optimize results.
